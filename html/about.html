Who We Are

A Neighborhood Café With Global Roots

AiKoFe was created in 2026, inspired by my older sister, whose nickname is Aiko. The name “AiKoFe” combines her nickname with “Kofe,” a unique spelling of “coffee,” her favorite drink. Since my sister loves coffee, I decided to create a website dedicated to coffee, inspired by her love for it. AiKoFe is a personal project that combines family inspiration with my passion for creating a coffee-themed website.

Today we serve thousands of cups a week, but every batch is still roasted in-house and every shot is still pulled by hand.

Our Philosophy

Coffee, Done Right

Respect the Bean

Direct-trade pricing and small-lot roasting to highlight each origin's character.

Craft Over Speed

Every dose weighed, every shot timed, grinder re-dialed throughout the day.

Community First

Local art on the walls, local pastries on the shelves, a table always open.
